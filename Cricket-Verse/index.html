Formats Of Cricket

Three major international formats, each with its own identity.

Test Cricket

The traditional format. A match can last up to five days, with two innings per team.

UP TO 5 DAYS

One Day International

A limited-overs international format in which each team normally receives 50 overs.

50 OVERS

T20 International

A fast-paced format where each team normally receives 20 overs.
